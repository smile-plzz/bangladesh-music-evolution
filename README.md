# The Evolution of Contemporary Bangladeshi Music Culture

**A Computational Analysis of Genre Development, Global Influences, Listener Communities, and Musical Ecosystems**

## Overview

This repository contains the research materials, data analysis scripts, frameworks, and findings for a comprehensive study on the evolution of Bangladesh's music culture from the late 1970s to the present.

### Research Title
From Folk Roots to Digital Streams: The Evolution of Bangladeshi Music Genres, Listener Cultures, and Global Musical Influences

### Core Focus
- Genre evolution (Mainstream Rock, Progressive Rock/Metal, Heavy Metal, Alternative/Indie, Pop, Hip-Hop/Rap, Folk & Folk Fusion)
- Global musical influences and local adaptation
- Listener preference networks
- Concert & community culture
- Industry transformation (cassette → streaming → AI)
- Future trends of the Bangladeshi music ecosystem

## Key Frameworks

### Bangladesh Musical Ecosystem Model (BMEM)
Integrates:
1. Historical Evolution
2. Global Influence
3. Local Adaptation
4. Listener Preference Networks
5. Music Experience Culture (Concerts)
6. Industry Transformation

### Bangladesh Music Preference Network (BMPN)
Graph connecting artists, genres, concerts, and listener communities through shared audiences, playlist co-occurrence, and social associations.

## Methodology Summary

**Approach**: Secondary data analysis + Computational Social Science / Digital Humanities

**Data Sources**:
- Streaming platforms (Spotify, YouTube Music)
- YouTube (views, engagement, comments)
- Social media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X)
- Concert archives (Facebook Events, ticketing platforms, university festivals)
- Historical sources (newspapers, magazines, Wikipedia, blogs)
- Artist interviews, album reviews, discographies

**Analysis Techniques**:
- Temporal analysis
- Network analysis & community detection
- Sentiment analysis (NLP on comments)
- Sound evolution analysis (across albums/eras)
- Concert ecosystem mapping

## Success Criteria

- Timeline covering 50+ years of Bangladeshi music development
- ≥300 Bangladeshi artists catalogued
- ≥500 publicly documented concerts and festivals analyzed
- Preference network graph connecting artists through shared audiences
- Identification of statistically meaningful listener ecosystems
- Visual genre evolution map
- Findings consistent across multiple independent online data sources
- Open dataset, analytical framework, visualizations, and final thesis
