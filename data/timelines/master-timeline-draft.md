# Bangladesh Music Evolution Timeline (Draft)

**Scope**: 1971–Present, with emphasis on band culture, genre emergence, technology, and industry shifts.  
**Status**: Working draft — to be expanded, verified, and cross-referenced with primary sources.

---

## Pre-1971 & Liberation Context
- Rich folk traditions (Baul, Bhatiali, Bhawaiya), Nazrul Geeti, Rabindra Sangeet, and emerging modern Bangla song forms form the cultural base.
- Political and linguistic identity struggles (Language Movement, Liberation War) shape the emotional and thematic landscape for subsequent popular music.

## Early 1970s – Foundations of Band Culture
- **Azam Khan / Uccharon**: Freedom-fighter-turned-musician; introduces rock-inflected, socially resonant popular music. Influential model for later bands.
- Early hotel/club performance circuit begins to form in Dhaka and Chittagong.

## Mid–Late 1970s – First Wave of Bands
| Year (approx.) | Event |
|----------------|-------|
| ~1972–73 | **Souls** formed in Chittagong (initially Surela) |
| 1976 | **Feedback** formed in Dhaka |
| 1978–79 | **Miles** formed; **Feelings** (later Nagar Baul / James) active |
| Late 1970s | Hotel and club gigs become regular; Western rock vocabulary enters local performance practice |

## 1980–1985 – Commercial Breakthrough of Band Albums
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1980–82 | **Souls** release *Super Souls* — widely regarded as a landmark early band album; hits such as "Mon Shudhu Mon Chuyechhe" become generational anthems |
| 1982 | **Miles** debut album |
| Mid-1980s | **Feedback** albums (*Ullash*, etc.); BAMBA (Bangladesh Musical Bands Association) foundations laid |
| 1984 | **Warfaze** formed |
| 1985 | **Rockstrata** formed; heavy metal cover culture begins to surface |

## Mid–Late 1980s – Heavy Metal Emerges
- Warfaze, Rockstrata, In Dhaka, Aces perform covers of Iron Maiden, Metallica, Deep Purple, Dokken, etc.
- Metal still largely underground / stigmatized; live shows build a dedicated youth audience.
- Parallel growth of hard rock and progressive-leaning experiments.

## 1990–1995 – Original Bangla Metal & Golden Cassette Era
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1991 | **Warfaze** self-titled debut album (Sargam) — "Boshe Achhi", "Ekti Chele" etc. establish original Bangla heavy metal |
| 1991 | **LRB (Love Runs Blind)** formed by Ayub Bachchu after leaving Souls |
| 1991 | **Ark** formed |
| 1992–94 | First public performances of original Bangla metal material gain traction (e.g., BAMBA / University of Dhaka shows) |
| 1994 | **Warfaze** *Obak Bhalobasha*; **Feedback** *Bongabdo 1400* (socially charged) |
| Mid-1990s | Cassette-era peak; rock/metal becomes a major popular force |

## Late 1990s – Diversification & Progressive Rise
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1993 | **Cryptic Fate** formed |
| 1996 | **Shironamhin**, **Dalchhut** formed |
| 1998–99 | **Aurthohin**, **Black**, **Artcell**, **Nemesis** emerge |
| 1999 | **Artcell** formed (official activity from Oct 1999) |

## 2000–2010 – Progressive Mainstream, Indie & Digital Transition
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2002 | **Artcell** *Onno Shomoy* — progressive metal gains wider recognition |
| 2006 | **Artcell** *Oniket Prantor* |
| 2000s | YouTube begins to transform discovery and distribution; independent and alternative scenes (Meghdol and others) grow |
| Mid–late 2000s | Decline of physical album dominance accelerates; live performance and digital visibility become more central |

## 2010s – Streaming, Social Media, Genre Pluralism
- Spotify and other streaming platforms expand reach.
- Hip-hop / rap scenes grow (Stoic Bliss, Muza, Hannan, Jalali Set and others).
- Folk-fusion and reinterpretation projects gain visibility (Arnob, Coke Studio Bangla collaborations).
- Indie and alternative continue as distinct ecosystems with intimate venue culture.
- Classic bands (Warfaze, James/Nagar Baul, Miles, LRB lineage) maintain multi-generational audiences through concerts and digital re-circulation.

## 2020s – Ecosystem Maturity & Recognition
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2023 | **Artcell** *Otritio* released; continued diaspora touring |
| 2020s | Concert culture remains vibrant (university festivals, open-air headliners, metal festivals, indie gigs) |
| 2026 | **Warfaze** awarded Ekushey Padak for contribution to rock music — landmark official recognition of a once-stigmatized genre |

---

## Cross-Cutting Technological / Industry Arc

```
Cassette era (1980s–90s)
    → CD & FM / TV music channels
        → YouTube & digital downloads (2000s)
            → Spotify & algorithmic streaming (2010s–)
                → Social media (Facebook, TikTok, Instagram) as discovery & community platforms
                    → Emerging AI-assisted production & recommendation (2020s)
```
